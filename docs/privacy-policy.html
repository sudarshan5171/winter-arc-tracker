Winter Arc Tracker

Privacy Policy • Effective Date: September 2026

Winter Arc Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled, stored, and protected when using our mobile application.

Key Summary: Winter Arc Tracker is built with an offline-first architecture. Your habits, streaks, and progress remain under your control and are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Store

When you use Winter Arc Tracker, the application processes the following types of information:

  • Habits and Goals: Custom habit titles, preset goals, units of measurement, and target numerical values.
  • Completion Records: Daily check-in timestamps, status completions, streaks, and calendar heatmap history.
  • App Preferences: Custom challenge dates, notification reminder times, and theme settings (Light/Dark mode).

2. Data Storage & Cloud Synchronization

  • Local Storage (Hive): All habit and streak information is encrypted and stored locally on your device's persistent storage.
  • Cloud Backup (Google Firebase): For users who utilize silent cloud backup, data is synced to Google Cloud Firestore under a randomly generated anonymous UID without collecting your name, phone number, or personal identity.

3. Device Permissions & Notifications

Winter Arc Tracker requests the following device permissions solely to support core features:

  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used exclusively to deliver your scheduled daily habit reminder alerts at your chosen time. We never send promotional messages or third-party ads.
  • Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): Used to trigger alarms at the exact designated minute, even when the device is locked or the app is closed.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs to facilitate cloud sync and notifications:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
  • Google Play Services

5. Data Retention & User Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can modify or delete individual habits at any time within the app.
  • You can erase all historical data and reset your Winter Arc by selecting "Reset All Arc & Streak Data" in the Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local database records from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Winter Arc Tracker does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
